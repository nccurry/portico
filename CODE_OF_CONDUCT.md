# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Portico welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat other people with respect. Keep discussions focused on the project and the
work.

Good conduct includes:

- Giving clear, useful feedback.
- Respecting different views and experiences.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Giving other people time and space to respond.

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated disruption.
- Sexual language, images, attention, or advances.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that is unsafe or abusive.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other Portico community spaces. It also applies when a person represents Portico
in public.

## Report a problem

Send conduct reports to [code@nickcurry.com](mailto:code@nickcurry.com). Do not
open a public issue. Include enough detail for the maintainer to understand what
happened.

The maintainer will review each report and protect the reporter's privacy as far
as practical. If a report concerns the maintainer, use
[GitHub's abuse report](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove content that breaks this code. The maintainer
can also issue a warning or restrict a person's access. Serious or repeated
conduct can result in a permanent ban from the project.

The response will depend on the conduct, its effect, and its history.
